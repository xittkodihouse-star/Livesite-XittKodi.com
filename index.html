Our Goan Kitchen Story

In Konkani, “Xitt Kodi” simply means rice and curry – the humble, everyday plate that keeps Goa going. At Xitt Kodi House we honour that plate with recipes that have travelled from village kitchens to our London stove.

Our masalas are ground fresh, our toddy-vinegar pickles are slow cured, and our seafood is cooked just until it tastes of the sea, not the stove. It is food that feels like Sunday lunch in Goa – shared, noisy and full of stories.

Whether you grew up on the Konkan coast or are tasting it for the first time, we want every meal here to feel like coming home.

Goan Favourites from Our Stove

Signature curry

Xitt Kodi

Goa’s soul on a plate – market-fresh fish simmered in a coconut-rich red curry, ladled over steaming rice.

Best enjoyed with a squeeze of lime and a lazy afternoon.

Coastal classic

Crab Xec Xec

Roasted coconut, Kashmiri chilli and coriander hug sweet crab claws in a thick, clinging masala.

Roll up your sleeves – this one is gloriously messy.

Pickled heat

Prawn Balchão

Tangy, spicy and addictive. Prawns cooked in a toddy-vinegar pickle with garlic, chilli and warmth that lingers.

Pairs beautifully with a cold drink and hot pav.

Fry-up favourite

Kingfish Rava Fry

Kingfish marinated in ginger-garlic and lime, coated in rava and pan-fried till crisp outside, juicy within.

Served with coconut chutney and salad.

From the tandoor

Chicken Cafreal

Green masala of coriander, chillies and spice coats chicken grilled till smoky, juicy and bright with flavour.

A Goan-Portuguese classic born on the streets of Goa.

Sweet ending

Bebinca Slice

Traditional layered Goan dessert made with coconut milk, ghee and patience, gently caramelised in every layer.

Finished with nutmeg and served warm with ice cream.
